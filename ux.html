Figma screen prototypes of my concept app for emailing.

Email App Prototypes

A Figma prototype for an email app called "PetalPost" that encourages users to reduce their digital carbon footprint through simple, engaging actions such as managing subscriptions, clearing unnecessary emails and visualising their impact in a calm and accessible way. I used visually pleasing colours and consistent designs to stay UX-friendly, as well as creating my own visuals using Adobe Illustrator.
